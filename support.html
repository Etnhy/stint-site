Questions and answers

I got a new iPhone — where is my data?

Sign in with the same Apple Account and keep iCloud on for Stint (Settings → your name → iCloud). On first launch Stint looks for your data in iCloud for a few seconds and brings your cars back. If sync has a problem, Stint shows it in Settings → Sync.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open Stint → Settings → Subscription → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel the subscription or the free trial?

On your iPhone open Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or Stint → Settings → Manage subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Stint reminds you two days before a trial ends.

Reminders don’t arrive

Allow notifications for Stint in iPhone Settings → Notifications. The reminder time is in Stint → Settings → Reminders. A plan needs a starting point: mark when you last did the service, then Stint can count the next date.

How do I move my log from Fuelio or Drivvo?

Export a CSV in your old app. In Stint open Settings → Data → Import from another app and pick the file. Duplicates are skipped, and the last import can be undone.

How do I back up or export my data?

Settings → Data → Export to CSV saves every log entry. A PDF history of a car (Pro) is in the car’s card. With iCloud on, everything is also kept in your private iCloud.

How do I delete my data?

Delete entries and cars in the app: a car’s card has Delete. Deleting the app removes its data from the iPhone; the copy in iCloud can be removed in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.
