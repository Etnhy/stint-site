Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Stint does not collect personal data. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run servers that receive your data.

What stays on your device

Your cars, refuels and other entries, odometer readings, service plans, reminders and receipt photos are stored in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. They are included in your device backups according to your iCloud or computer backup settings. We cannot access them.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, Stint keeps a copy of your data in your private iCloud database so it appears on all your devices and survives reinstalling the app. This copy is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and is protected by it; we have no access to it. You can turn off iCloud for Stint in Settings → Apple Account → iCloud.

Camera and photos

Stint uses the camera or the photo picker only when you add a receipt photo. The photo picker gives the app access only to the photos you choose. Photos are compressed and kept on your device together with the entry.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No push servers are involved.

Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts

Widgets read a short summary that the app writes to a container shared only between Stint and its widgets on your device. Siri and Shortcuts requests are processed by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; Stint receives only the values from your request, such as an amount or an odometer reading.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. The app checks your subscription status through Apple’s StoreKit on the device.

Exports

CSV and PDF files are created on your device and are shared only where you choose to send them.

Deleting your data

You can delete entries and cars in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device; data in iCloud is removed when you delete it in the app or in iCloud storage settings.

Children

Stint is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
